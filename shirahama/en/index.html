No frills. Just the sea, a hot spring, and experienced hands.

Our salon is a single apartment in Shirahama, a hot-spring town on Japan's Pacific coast, right by Engetsu Island. In front of the building is a quiet beach inside the bay.

We host only one group a day, so the whole space, and our full attention, is yours. We don't do fancy décor. What we offer is a warm, homey place and therapists with many years of hands-on experience.

No rushing, no schedule to watch. Just a slow day with the sea, the onsen and good care.

Getting here: about 15 minutes by car from Nanki-Shirahama Airport or JR Shirahama Station. Free parking at the building. East and West, in one approach

At D-SHIRAHAMA, we read your constitution through traditional Chinese medicine, blend aromatherapy oils to suit it, and work mainly along the meridians. After the treatment, we share food-based self-care drawing on hormone-immune nutrition and Chinese medicinal cooking, so the care carries on in your daily life at home.

We believe beauty grows out of good health. That is why EIKO's beauty care, such as face-line and lymphatic treatments, is part of the same approach as caring for your body from the inside.

Our aim is integrated care that brings together two kinds of knowledge: Chinese medicine from the East and nutrition science from the West. Our long-term goal is to work with medical institutions and build a place for integrative medicine.

Our current treatments are for relaxation. They are not medical treatment.

How your day goes

01

Morning · Sea breeze (optional)

Start slowly on the quiet beach in front of the salon. On fine summer days, you can also go into the sea. Thalassotherapy is the practice of using the sea, its water and its air, to care for the body. It has dedicated centres in France and around the world, and research continues. Seawater contains magnesium, a mineral that plays a part in how our muscles work. Following this idea, we treat time in the sea as a way to let the body loosen up before your treatment. If you plan to go in, just bring your swimwear. We lend towels, swim rings and floats. After the sea, you can rinse off sand and salt at the outdoor shower by the building. If you would like to try stand-up paddleboarding or other activities, we can introduce the tours run by the dive shop next door. You are also welcome to arrive at 1 pm.

02

Midday · Lunch

Lunch at a local restaurant nearby, with food from the area. Lunch is not included in the price.

03

1:00 pm · Eastern medicine check-in

In the way of traditional Chinese medicine, we look at your pulse, tongue and complexion, and talk about how you have been feeling.

04

1:30 pm · The treatment (3 hours or more)

In the sea-view living room, we begin with a foot bath, then work over the whole body, mainly along the meridians, with aromatherapy oils blended for your constitution, combined with a mugwort steam (yomogi-mushi), a far-infrared dome sauna, cupping and warming care. We set the length to suit your body that day, and it is never shorter than three hours.

05

After the treatment · Onsen and wind-down

Soak in the building's natural hot-spring bath. Then an after-care talk on food-based self-care, drawing on hormone-immune nutrition and Chinese medicinal cooking, with simple habits to take home. In some seasons, you can watch the sun set near Engetsu Island. The day ends after 6 pm, so if you are coming from far away, we recommend staying overnight in Shirahama.

Treatment at your hotel

We can also come to your hotel room. The Chinese medicine consultation and the treatment of three hours or more are the same as at the salon, and afterwards you can simply rest in your room.

The price is the same as at the salon. There is no travel fee for hotels in Shirahama town; for hotels outside the town, please ask us first.

The full experience, including the mugwort steam and dome sauna, is at the salon. If you are not sure which suits you, just ask us.

  • Your room needs space for a treatment bed (two beds for 2 × 2).
  • The mugwort steam and far-infrared dome sauna are too large to bring, so they are not part of hotel visits. Please enjoy your hotel's own hot spring.
  • Please check with your hotel in advance that therapists may visit your room, and that there is parking for our car (we bring the treatment bed and supplies by car).

Your therapists

Pearl Sakamoto

A therapist grounded in traditional Chinese medicine. He reads each guest's constitution through bianzheng lunzhi, the TCM method of pattern differentiation, blends aromatherapy oils to match it, and centres the treatment on the meridians, choosing oils with their meridian affinities in mind. He actively takes in ideas from other schools and keeps refining his work. Beyond the treatment, he guides food-based self-care drawing on hormone-immune nutrition and Chinese medicinal cooking. He runs the Ninkatsu Advisor Association, a non-profit for people preparing for pregnancy, and keeps learning by attending academic conferences. He is also a licensed registered seller of over-the-counter medicines in Japan. Founded Bikyaku Shokunin in Osaka in 2012, a women-only aroma salon with male therapists, and runs a training school for therapists. Looked after models backstage at GirlsAward, one of Japan's biggest fashion events, for three years from 2013. Founder of the Male Therapist Grand Prix and chair of the Japan Male Therapist Association.

EIKO (Eiko Watase)

Runs Resalute, a private esthetic salon in Yoyogi, Tokyo. A qualified dietitian, she has run her own salon since 2012. Also teaches and works as a salon management consultant. Known for lymphatic drainage, aroma care and face-line treatments. As a dietitian, she also gives food advice based on nutrigenomics.

Questions

How do I book?

Yes. We offer free pick-up and drop-off from JR Shirahama Station, Nanki-Shirahama Airport and hotels in Shirahama town. Just tell us your arrival time and place when you book.

It is possible, but the day ends after 6 pm, so if you are coming from far away, we recommend staying overnight in Shirahama.

Just bring your swimwear. We lend towels, swim rings and floats. After the sea, you can rinse off sand and salt at the outdoor shower by the building. Yes. We can give the treatment in your hotel room. The price is the same as at the salon, with no travel fee within Shirahama town. Your room needs space for a treatment bed, and please check with your hotel in advance that therapists may visit and that there is parking for our car.
Can we communicate in English?
Yes. Feel free to write to us in English.
When do I pay?
You pay a deposit of half the price when you book, by card or another method through a secure payment link. You pay the rest on the day.
Can I cancel?
Up to 30 days before your visit, we refund the deposit in full. See the booking policy for later cancellations.
I would prefer a therapist of my own gender.
No problem at all. Just let us know when you book.
Is anyone unable to take the treatment?
If you are pregnant, or being treated for a heart or blood pressure condition, the treatment may not be suitable. Please ask us before booking.
