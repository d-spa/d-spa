Booking policy

Deposit and payment

  • When you book, you pay half of the price as a deposit: ¥44,400 for 1×1, ¥88,800 for 1×2 or 2×2.
  • The deposit is paid by card or another method through a payment link we send you (Stripe).
  • You pay the rest on the day. We will tell you how when you book.
  • Lunch and travel are not included in the price.

Cancellation and date changes

  • Up to 30 days before your visit: full refund of the deposit.
  • 29 to 15 days before: half of the deposit is refunded.
  • 14 days before or later: no refund.
  • You can change the date once for free if you tell us at least 15 days before.
  • If transport is stopped by a typhoon or similar, or if we have to cancel, we refund the deposit in full.
  • Refunds go back to the card or method you paid with.

Health

  • Please ask us before booking if any of the following applies. The treatment may not be suitable.
  • You are pregnant, or may be pregnant
  • You are being treated for a heart condition, blood pressure or a similar condition
  • A doctor has told you to avoid hot baths or saunas
  • You have a fever or feel unwell
  • You have had alcohol that day
  • You have inflamed or broken skin
  • We check how you feel on the day and may adjust the treatment.
  • Our treatments are for relaxation. They are not medical treatment.

Time in the sea

  • Going into the sea is only on fine summer days, and only if you wish. We may call it off depending on the weather and the sea.
  • The beach in front of the salon has no lifeguards. Please enjoy the sea at your own judgement and responsibility.
  • For stand-up paddleboarding and other activities, we introduce the tours run by the dive shop next door (paid separately).

Privacy

  • We use your name, contact details, booking details and health information only to manage your booking and to give you a safe treatment.
  • We ask about your health only with your consent.
  • We do not share your information with anyone else without your consent, except where the law requires it.
  • Payments go through Stripe. We never receive your card number.
  • This website does not use analytics or cookies.
  • To see, correct or delete your information, contact us below.
