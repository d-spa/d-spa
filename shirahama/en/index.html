Message us on Instagram

No frills. Just the sea, a hot spring, and experienced hands.

Our salon is a single apartment in Shirahama, a hot-spring town on Japan's Pacific coast, right by Engetsu Island. In front of the building is a quiet beach inside the bay.

We host only one group a day, so the whole space, and our full attention, is yours. We don't do fancy décor. What we offer is a warm, homey place and therapists with many years of hands-on experience.

No rushing, no schedule to watch. Just a slow day with the sea, the onsen and good care.

Getting here: about 15 minutes by car from Nanki-Shirahama Airport or JR Shirahama Station. Free parking at the building. Shirahama is about 2.5 hours from Osaka by limited express train, or about 1 hour by air from Tokyo Haneda. We share the exact location once your booking is confirmed.

How your day goes

01

Morning · Sea breeze (optional)

Start slowly on the quiet beach in front of the salon. On fine summer days, you can also go into the sea. If you would like to try stand-up paddleboarding or other activities, we can introduce the tours run by the dive shop next door. You are also welcome to arrive at 1 pm.

02

Midday · Lunch

Lunch at a local restaurant nearby, with food from the area. Lunch is not included in the price.

03

1:00 pm · Eastern medicine check-in

In the way of traditional Chinese medicine, we look at your pulse, tongue and complexion, and talk about how you have been feeling.

04

1:30 pm · The treatment (3 hours or more)

In the sea-view living room, we begin with a foot bath, then work on the lymph and meridians of the whole body with aromatherapy oils, combined with a mugwort steam (yomogi-mushi), a far-infrared dome sauna, cupping and warming care. We set the length to suit your body that day, and it is never shorter than three hours.

05

After the treatment · Onsen and wind-down

Soak in the building's natural hot-spring bath. Then a short chat about Eastern herbal food and nutrition, with simple tips to take home. In some seasons, you can watch the sun set near Engetsu Island. The day ends after 6 pm, so if you are coming from far away, we recommend staying overnight in Shirahama.

East and West, in one approach

At D-SHIRAHAMA, we read your constitution through traditional Chinese medicine, blend aromatherapy oils to suit it, and work mainly along the meridians. After the treatment, we share food-based self-care drawing on hormone-immune nutrition and Chinese medicinal cooking, so the care carries on in your daily life at home.

Our aim is integrated care that brings together two kinds of knowledge: Chinese medicine from the East and nutrition science from the West. Our long-term goal is to work with medical institutions and build a place for integrative medicine.

Our current treatments are for relaxation. They are not medical treatment.

Built on yin and yang

Our approach comes from the idea of yin and yang in Chinese medicine. As a basic rule, a male therapist (yang) works with women, who tend toward yin, and a female therapist (yin) works with men, who tend toward yang. If you would feel more comfortable with a therapist of your own gender, just tell us. That is always fine.

When two of you come together, we pair the therapists in the same way.

Your therapists

Pearl Sakamoto

A therapist grounded in traditional Chinese medicine. He reads each guest's constitution through bianzheng lunzhi, the TCM method of pattern differentiation, blends aromatherapy oils to match it, and centres the treatment on the meridians, choosing oils with their meridian affinities in mind. He actively takes in ideas from other schools and keeps refining his work. Beyond the treatment, he guides food-based self-care drawing on hormone-immune nutrition and Chinese medicinal cooking. Questions

How do I book?
Send us a DM on Instagram or a message on LINE. Messaging is the easiest way for us to help you in English.
Can I do it as a day trip?
It is possible, but the day ends after 6 pm, so if you are coming from far away, we recommend staying overnight in Shirahama.
Can we communicate in English?
Yes. Feel free to write to us in English.
When do I pay?
You pay a deposit of half the price when you book, by card or another method through a secure payment link. You pay the rest on the day.
Can I cancel?
Up to 30 days before your visit, we refund the deposit in full. See the booking policy for later cancellations.
I would prefer a therapist of my own gender.
No problem at all. Just let us know when you book.
Is anyone unable to take the treatment?
If you are pregnant, or being treated for a heart or blood pressure condition, the treatment may not be suitable. Please ask us before booking.
